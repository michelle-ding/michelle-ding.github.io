## Research
### AI-Generated Non-Consensual Intimate Images
My work aims to translate across technical, policy, and advocacy domains to push for survivor-centered AIG-NCII prevention. My current [research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04759) maps out the technological ecosystem powering generative AI abuse, serving as a taxonomy and resource for stakeholders in policy, academia, industry, and civil society. My work has been cited by the [International AI Safety Report](https://internationalaisafetyreport.org/publication/international-ai-safety-report-2026), [Berkman Klein Center](https://cyber.harvard.edu/publication/2026/mapping-burden-survivor-centered-reporting-systems-policies-ncii), [Thorn](https://aichildsafety.github.io/), and recent [class action](https://cdn.arstechnica.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Doe-v-xAI-Amended-Complaint-7-7-26.pdf) against xAI. I spoke about my research in [Decode's](https://www.decodeinternet.in/life/investigation/desi-faces-foreign-servers-inside-the-ai-economy-undressing-women-31691) investigative piece exposing the AI nudifier economy in India. I am also an author in the 2026 [Oxford Handbook on Human Security](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/63026/chapter/566098097). I draw from my past experience as an [advocate for sexual violence prevention](https://www.browndailyherald.com/article/2022/11/an-ongoing-effort-looking-into-past-present-future-of-sexual-assault-activism-at-brown) and as a sexual assault peer educator at Brown.

### Community-Driven LLM Evaluations & Socio-Technical AI Governance
My goal is to empower impacted communities to evaluate and govern AI systems for themselves. I am currently leading a study in collaboration with the [Re/Imagination Lab](https://www.reimaginationlab.org/) that evaluates LLM outputs for accuracy of abortion-access information across 9 states in the U.S. in English and Spanish. I am also exploring methods for researchers to conduct LLM evaluations with robust location signals. For more of my thoughts on governance & evaluations, read my op-eds [Coding Isn't Everything](https://michellelding.substack.com/p/coding-isnt-everything) and [Imagining Dystopias](https://cntr.brown.edu/news/2025-11-19/imagining-dystopias).
